Everyday nutrition basics

🥦 Half your plate: plants

Vegetables and fruit add fibre, vitamins and volume. Aim for a variety of colours through the week.

🌾 Choose whole grains

Oats, brown rice, whole-wheat bread and legumes give steadier energy than refined grains.

🐟 Quality protein

Fish, eggs, poultry, beans, lentils and tofu help you feel full and support muscle.

🫒 Healthier fats

Olive oil, nuts, seeds and oily fish beat fried and heavily processed foods.

💧 Stay hydrated

Water is the best everyday drink. Go easy on sugary drinks and juices.

🕒 Regular meals

Eating at roughly the same times each day supports steady energy, mood and sleep.

Some medications can affect appetite, thirst or weight. If you take any, check with your doctor or pharmacist before making big changes to your diet.

Popular eating patterns

🫒 Mediterranean

Vegetables, legumes, olive oil, fish and whole grains, with little red meat.

🧂 DASH

🥑 Lower-carb

Fewer starchy and sugary foods, more protein and fats. Quality of food still matters.

Needs care

The best pattern is one you can keep up. Some health conditions and medications are sensitive to changes in salt, fluids or weight, so check with a doctor or dietitian before making big changes.
