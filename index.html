BMI is a quick screening tool, not a diagnosis. It doesn’t account for muscle mass, age, sex or body shape. For personal advice, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian.

Everyday nutrition basics

Some medications can affect appetite, thirst or weight. If you take any, check with your doctor or pharmacist before making big changes to your diet.

Popular eating patterns

The best pattern is one you can keep up. Some health conditions and medications are sensitive to changes in salt, fluids or weight, so check with a doctor or dietitian before making big changes.
